# MAYTHA rules

- **Design:** premium, consistent and mobile-first. Reuse existing components and the tokens in `src/app/globals.css`; do not copy another brand's identity.
- **Languages:** Arabic RTL first, English LTR; use logical properties. Meet accessibility basics (labels, contrast, 44px targets).
- **Facts:** products, prices and variants come from the catalog. Never invent product claims, discounts or results.
- **Business rules:** preserve checkout, inventory and delivery (Maythaloun only: 5 ₪ under 100 ₪, free from 100 ₪, cash on delivery).
- **Ask first:** database changes, Production configuration or data, new dependencies, publishing, merging, deploying. GitHub is read-only unless asked.
- **Skills, on demand:** `web-design-guidelines`, `maytha-e2e`; invoke `/design-taste-frontend`, `/maytha-image-to-code`, `/maytha-security-review` when needed. The rules here override any skill's style or process.
